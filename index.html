WHAT WE DO

Services built around your business, not a template

From statutory audits to hands-on financial analytics, every engagement starts with understanding what you actually need.

Audit & Assurance

Independent, objective audits that give lenders, investors and regulators confidence in your financial statements — and give you an honest read on your own operations.

  • Statutory & external audits
  • Internal control reviews
  • Financial statement assurance
  • NGO & donor-funded project audits

Tax Consulting & Compliance

Strategic tax planning and reliable compliance support so you meet every obligation on time, while legally minimizing your tax burden.

  • KRA iTax registration & filing
  • VAT, PAYE & income tax returns
  • Tax health checks & dispute support
  • Tax-efficient business structuring

Financial Analytics

Your data holds answers. We turn raw figures into dashboards, models and reports that make the next decision obvious.

  • Financial modelling & forecasting
  • KPI dashboards & reporting
  • Data cleaning & analysis (Excel & beyond)
  • Business intelligence support

Business Advisory

Practical, judgement-led guidance for owners who want a sounding board that understands both the numbers and the business behind them.

  • Budgeting & cash-flow planning
  • Business registration & setup guidance
  • Growth & performance reviews
  • Board & investor reporting support
